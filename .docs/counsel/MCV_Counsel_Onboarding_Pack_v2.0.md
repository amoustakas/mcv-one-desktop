## 2.5 Design Principles

The architecture and scope of this Pack are governed by a small set of founder-authored design principles. These are not implementation details; they are posture choices that shape what counsel is being asked to do.

1. **Architecture first, legal second.** The founders design the architecture; counsel gives it legal form. Counsel is not being asked to invent a structure — counsel is being asked to validate, form, and refine a structure that has been worked through.
2. **Civilization vs. wealth separation.** The founders are stewards, not owners, of the civilization-layer IP. They sit architecturally **below** the platform: copyrights are held by Moose Caliber Ventures Inc. (an entity that will itself be held by the Singapore VCC as a subsidiary) until assigned upward to Root. This is deliberate. The founders never personally hold the IP at any point.
3. **Contamination prevention.** A failure at Layer 2 (e.g., a single venture's litigation or regulatory action) cannot reach Layer 1 or Layer 0. This principle shapes the entity-formation sequencing in Section 5 and the sublicense architecture in Section 3.5.
4. **Scale for 8 billion.** Every architectural decision is stress-tested against an 8-billion-user ceiling. This is not fantasy; it is the design horizon, and it shapes decisions about jurisdictional routing, agent-identity infrastructure, and protocol-level transfer restrictions.
5. **Full scope, no deferrals.** The budget scope posture in Section 1.5 is locked at full pipeline (P0 + P1 + P2 + Madrid + utility conversion). The founders have deliberated and are committing to the full envelope rather than scoping down. Counsel is asked to scope against full pipeline.

Each layer is formally independent; the relationships between layers are (i) sublicense from Root to MCV Tech (Ireland DAC), (ii) intercompany IP licenses from MCV Tech to Layer 2 ventures, and (iii) economic relationships (equity, revenue-share) between Layer 1 and Layer 2 per venture.

## 3.2 Layer 0 — Root (IP Custody Apex)

**Prior draft name: "The Covenant."** Renamed to Root across all current documents.

Root is a Purpose Trust established in a Commonwealth offshore jurisdiction. Counsel is asked to recommend the specific jurisdiction based on the following criteria, ranked:

1. **Purpose Trust statute maturity.** Jersey (1984, amended), Guernsey (2007), and Cayman (STAR Trust, 1997) all have established purpose-trust regimes. Counsel to advise on which best supports a centennial-horizon IP-custody purpose.
2. **Enforceability of non-charitable purposes.** The trust's purpose is to hold and protect the civilizational-layer IP of the MCV Universe, including authorizing commercially reasonable licensing into Layer 1 operating entities. The purpose is non-charitable.
3. **Separation of enforcer, trustee, and beneficiary roles.** The design contemplates a professional trustee, a separately-appointed enforcer (to police purpose fidelity), and a beneficiary class consisting of the Layer 1 MCV Holdings Ltd and — indirectly, through the ecosystem — the user base of the platform.
4. **Reporting and transparency regime.** Root will produce annual transparency statements that are published to the ecosystem.

Root holds: the foundational codex documents, the patents (upon assignment), the constitutional trademarks (MCV, MCV UNIVERSE, ROOT, and related), the copyrights on all foundational architectural works. These are licensed into Layer 1 (specifically into MCV Tech, Ireland DAC) on commercially reasonable terms, with a royalty flow-back designed to fund Root's ongoing operations and a permanent reserve.

**Timing:** counsel to advise on formation timeline. The founders' working assumption is that Root is formed after Layer 1 Singapore VCC is formed (sequence: MCV Holdings Ltd → MCV Tech Ireland DAC → Root). The IP assignment from Moose Caliber Ventures Inc. to Root occurs via an assignment deed executed as part of the trust-formation sequence.

**Interim posture:** Moose Caliber Ventures Inc. holds all copyrights and filed trademark applications on behalf of Root. Upon Root formation and MCV Inc.'s transfer into the Layer 1 VCC as a subsidiary, IP assigns from MCV Inc. to Root. The founders **never personally hold** the IP.

## 3.3 Layer 1 — The MCV Federation (Coordinator)

Layer 1 is the coordination layer. It aggregates venture revenue, manages capital deployment, maintains the shared platform infrastructure (identity, economy, governance, observability), and owns the MCV brand. The apex entity is **MCV Holdings Ltd**, a Singapore Variable Capital Company (VCC).

### Singapore VCC Rationale

Singapore VCC offers specific advantages for a federated multi-venture structure: sub-fund segregation (each venture can be held in a separate segregated sub-fund under the same umbrella), statutory recognition of multiple classes of interest, favorable tax treatment under Singapore's income-tax regime, and a developed professional-services infrastructure. The VCC's use is most familiar in the fund-management context but is legitimate for closed-ended holding structures.

Alternative jurisdictions considered and rejected by the founders: Delaware LLC (insufficient for international scale), Cayman (conflicts with Root jurisdiction preference), BVI (political/reputational posture), Luxembourg (overhead-heavy for this stage). **Counsel is invited to push back on Singapore VCC** if counsel believes a different structure better fits the scope.

### Regional Operating Subsidiaries

Layer 1 holds five regional operating subsidiaries, each with a clear role:

| Entity | Jurisdiction | Role | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moose Caliber Ventures Inc.** | Ontario, Canada | Canadian operations + interim IP holder pre-Root | P0 (reinstated) |
| **MCV Tech** | Ireland (DAC) | IP sublicensee from Root; licenses IP out to Layer 2 ventures; royalty conduit | P0 |
| **MCV Finance Group** | Singapore (VCC) | Token treasury; securities issuance vehicle | P0 |
| **EdgeIQ Holdings Inc.** | Wyoming, USA | US operating entity; BetEdge AI parent | Existing |
| **MCV UAE Ltd** | UAE (ADGM) | Middle East market access; regulatory arbitrage pathway | P2 |
| **MCV EU** | Ireland or Luxembourg | EU market access | P2 |

**MCV Tech's role is critical.** Ireland DAC is chosen specifically because (i) Irish IP licensing benefits from the Knowledge Development Box regime, (ii) Ireland is an EU member providing EU-passporting for licensing structures, and (iii) a DAC provides shareholder transparency suitable for a regulated IP holder. MCV Tech is the only Layer 1 entity that holds IP — it sublicenses from Root upward and licenses to Layer 2 downward.

## 3.5 IP Flow and Sublicense Architecture

```
  Root (Layer 0)
    │
    ├─ holds: patents, copyrights, constitutional trademarks
    │
    │  sublicenses (perpetual, royalty-bearing, sublicensable)
    │   to:
    ▼
  MCV Tech (Ireland DAC)     [Layer 1]
    │
    │  licenses (field-of-use limited, royalty-bearing, revocable)
    │   to:
    ▼
  ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
  ▼                 ▼                 ▼                 ▼
  FutureState LP    BetEdge AI Inc    MCV Studios       (etc.)
  (Cayman)          (Wyoming)         (TBD)
  │                 │                 │
  │  uses IP to     │                 │
  │  operate        │                 │
  │  venture        │                 │
  ▼                 ▼                 ▼
  Royalty back up to MCV Tech → back up to Root
```

This architecture allows:
- Layer 2 litigation or regulatory action does not reach Layer 1 or Layer 0
- Individual ventures can be sold, spun off, wound down, or replaced without disturbing the IP chain
- Royalty flow creates a reliable funding mechanism for Root's century-scale operations
- Licensing terms can be customized per venture (e.g., FutureState's RWA-specific patent claims vs. BetEdge's ACS claims)
