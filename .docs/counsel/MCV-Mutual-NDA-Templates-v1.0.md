## IMPORTANT — Read Before Using

This document contains **founder-drafted starting templates** for review and refinement by qualified legal counsel. It is not legal advice. Specifically:

- **Do not execute either template without counsel review.** Tony is not a lawyer. Claude is not a lawyer. These templates are a starting point that counsel should review, refine, and approve before any external signature.
- **Counsel review is a deliverable inside engagement task IP-6** (see Pack v2.0 §6.2). The intent of these templates is to reduce counsel drafting time by presenting a complete working draft rather than a blank page.
- **Jurisdictional provisions** (governing law, dispute resolution, notice addresses) need counsel validation for each counterparty's jurisdiction.
- **Specific survival periods, non-circumvention tails, and remedy scope** should be reviewed against the counterparty's expected posture — an accredited investor engagement may land on different terms than a strategic-partner engagement.
- **MCV Universe corporate disclosure language** (about which entity signs, the capacity of the signatory, and the protected-party definition) needs counsel finalization once the Layer 1 Singapore VCC is formed. Until then, **Moose Caliber Ventures Inc. (Ontario)** is the signing party on MCV's side, acting on behalf of itself and its affiliates.

**Both templates include:**
- Mutual (two-way) confidentiality obligations
- 7-year survival on trade-secret-marked materials (longer than the base term)
- Mandatory flow-down to advisors and affiliates (the Representatives concept)
- Non-circumvention (6-month tail for Template A, 12-month for Template B)
- Canadian + Delaware governing-law **elective** (first-signer chooses)
- DMCA-aligned inadvertent-disclosure handling
- Injunctive relief + attorney's-fees provisions
- Notices by email (with hard-copy backup on critical matters)

# TEMPLATE A — CAPITAL / INVESTOR MUTUAL NDA

**Purpose of this template:** to enable confidential evaluation of an investment opportunity in FutureState or another venture within the MCV Universe. Narrow-scope. 2-year term. Not appropriate for partnerships or commercial collaborations — use Template B for those.

## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] (the "Effective Date"), by and between:**

**Moose Caliber Ventures Inc.**, an Ontario corporation with its principal office at [ADDRESS] ("MCV"), acting on its own behalf and on behalf of its affiliates and subsidiaries (collectively, the "MCV Parties"),

**and**

**[Counterparty Legal Name]**, a [entity type] with its principal office at [ADDRESS] (the "Counterparty"), acting on its own behalf and on behalf of its affiliates and subsidiaries.

Each of MCV and Counterparty is referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**." For purposes of this Agreement, each Party is both a Disclosing Party and a Receiving Party with respect to Confidential Information it shares and receives.

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to engage in confidential discussions relating to a potential investment by Counterparty in the FutureState venture, the MCV Universe platform economy, or a related investment vehicle sponsored by an MCV Party (the "**Purpose**"). In the course of these discussions, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The Parties enter into this Agreement to protect that Confidential Information.

### 2. Definitions

**"Confidential Information"** means any information disclosed by a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party that is either (i) marked or identified as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or similar designation at the time of disclosure; (ii) orally disclosed and confirmed in writing within thirty (30) days as confidential; or (iii) reasonably understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information expressly includes, without limitation:
- Architectural, technical, and product specifications of the MCV Universe and its ventures
- Financial information, capitalization tables, deal terms, and partner identities
- Token structures, tokenomics, smart-contract designs, and related economic models
- Business plans, strategies, product roadmaps, and go-to-market plans
- Identities of principals, partners, investors, and counterparties (including "Principal A" and "Principal B" referenced in certain MCV materials pending formal disclosure)
- Intellectual property including trade secrets, trademarks pending clearance or filing, patent candidates, and copyrighted materials
- Any document marked "Tier 0," "Tier 1," or "Tier 2" under the MCV tiered disclosure architecture

**"Trade-Secret-Marked Information"** means any Confidential Information explicitly marked as "TRADE SECRET" or "CONFIDENTIAL — TRADE SECRET" at the time of disclosure. Trade-Secret-Marked Information receives enhanced protection under Section 6 of this Agreement.

**"Representatives"** of a Receiving Party means that Party's directors, officers, employees, affiliates, professional advisors (attorneys, accountants, financial advisors), and consultants who have a legitimate need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

**"Permitted Use"** means review and evaluation of the potential investment referenced in Section 1. Permitted Use expressly does not include use for any competitive, commercial, or other purpose outside the Purpose.

### 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Permitted Use;

(b) protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a reasonable standard of care;

(c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party except to its Representatives, and only to those Representatives who (i) have a legitimate need to know for the Permitted Use and (ii) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement;

(d) be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, treating such breach as a breach by the Receiving Party itself;

(e) not reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or otherwise attempt to derive the structure, composition, or implementation of any software, system, or mechanism comprising Confidential Information;

(f) not file any patent, trademark, or copyright application based on, derived from, or incorporating Confidential Information;

(g) upon written request from the Disclosing Party, return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and certify in writing that it has done so, subject to customary record-retention exceptions for professional advisors.

### 4. Exclusions

The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written record:

(a) was in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, without obligation of confidentiality;
(b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;
(c) is received by the Receiving Party from a third party who is not bound by confidentiality obligations to the Disclosing Party; or
(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

### 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is legally compelled by subpoena, court order, or other legal process to disclose Confidential Information, it shall:

(a) promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing (to the extent legally permitted) to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other remedy;
(b) reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain such protective remedy, at the Disclosing Party's expense;
(c) disclose only the specific Confidential Information required and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve confidential treatment of any disclosure.

### 6. Term and Survival

This Agreement shall remain in effect for **two (2) years** from the Effective Date (the "**Term**"). The confidentiality obligations in Sections 3 and 5 survive termination or expiration of this Agreement:

- for a period of **three (3) years** after termination or expiration, with respect to Confidential Information generally; and
- for a period of **seven (7) years** after termination or expiration, with respect to Trade-Secret-Marked Information; and
- indefinitely with respect to any information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, for so long as such information remains a trade secret.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

### 7. Ownership and Reservation of Rights

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other right to use Confidential Information is granted by this Agreement, except as strictly necessary for the Permitted Use. No Party makes any representation or warranty regarding the accuracy, completeness, or non-infringement of any Confidential Information.

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into any subsequent agreement, investment, partnership, or transaction.

### 8. Non-Circumvention

For a period of **six (6) months** after the Effective Date, Counterparty agrees not to, directly or indirectly:

(a) contact, solicit, or transact with any principal, partner, investor, developer, vendor, or counterparty of an MCV Party (including Principal A and Principal B, if disclosed) whose identity was first learned by Counterparty through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement, for the purpose of entering into a transaction that would bypass or circumvent the MCV Parties; or

(b) make an investment in, acquire rights in, or enter into a financing transaction with respect to any specific asset or opportunity (including Cliff Bay) first learned of by Counterparty through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of the relevant MCV Party.

This Section 8 applies mutually, with appropriate substitution of counterparty names, to any principal, partner, investor, or counterparty of Counterparty first learned of by MCV through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement.

### 9. Inadvertent Disclosure

If a Receiving Party becomes aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information by itself or any Representative, it shall:

(a) promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing;
(b) take all reasonable steps to prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure;
(c) reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party to investigate and remediate the unauthorized use or disclosure.

Inadvertent disclosure of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or its Representatives does not waive the Disclosing Party's rights or constitute consent to further disclosure, provided the Receiving Party complies with this Section 9.

### 10. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the requirement to post bond. The prevailing Party in any action to enforce this Agreement shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

### 11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**Governing Law Election.** The Party first signing this Agreement (the "First Signer") elects one of the following governing-law options by initialing the applicable option below. The unelected option(s) shall be deleted from the executed version.

The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Ontario for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

- [ ] **Option 2 — Delaware, USA.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, USA, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

### 12. Miscellaneous

**Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, understandings, and agreements.

**Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

**Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that an MCV Party may assign this Agreement to (i) any affiliate of an MCV Party or (ii) a successor entity in connection with the formation of the Layer 0 or Layer 1 entities described in the MCV Universe Counsel Engagement Onboarding Pack, upon written notice to Counterparty.

**Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the unenforceable provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to render it enforceable.

**Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and delivered electronically (DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, or similar). Electronic signatures are binding.

**Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in writing to the addresses specified on the signature page, with copies by email to the designated email addresses. Notices become effective on actual receipt.

**No Publicity.** Neither Party shall issue any press release, public announcement, or disclosure identifying the other Party as a current or prospective counterparty, without the prior written consent of the other Party.

**No Employment or Agency.** This Agreement does not create an employment, agency, joint venture, or partnership relationship between the Parties.

**Counterparty Representation.** Counterparty represents that the individual signing this Agreement has the authority to bind Counterparty.

**Purpose of this template:** to enable confidential evaluation of a commercial partnership, acquisition, joint venture, or deep collaboration involving platform-level IP and roadmap. ## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT — STRATEGIC PARTNER

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] (the "Effective Date"), by and between:**

**Moose Caliber Ventures Inc.**, an Ontario corporation with its principal office at [ADDRESS] ("MCV"), acting on its own behalf and on behalf of its affiliates, subsidiaries, and successor entities (including entities to be formed pursuant to the three-layer architecture described in the MCV Universe Counsel Engagement Onboarding Pack v2.0, namely Root, MCV Holdings Ltd (Singapore VCC), MCV Tech (Ireland DAC), MCV Finance Group VCC, FutureState LP, and related venture entities) (collectively, the "**MCV Parties**"),

**and**

**[Counterparty Legal Name]**, a [entity type] with its principal office at [ADDRESS] (the "Counterparty"), acting on its own behalf and on behalf of its affiliates, subsidiaries, and successor entities.

Each of MCV and Counterparty is referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to engage in confidential discussions regarding a potential strategic partnership, joint venture, acquisition, commercial collaboration, or other deep commercial relationship involving the MCV Universe and one or more of its ventures, including without limitation FutureState real-world-asset tokenization initiatives (the "**Purpose**"). The Purpose contemplates mutual exchange of Confidential Information at a significantly greater scope and depth than a typical investor diligence process, including but not limited to platform architecture, cross-venture IP, long-term roadmap, and partnership structure for flagship assets.

### 2. Definitions

**"Confidential Information"** has the meaning set forth in Section 2 of Template A above, and additionally includes, without limitation:
- The three-layer corporate architecture (Root, Federation, Ventures) and all sub-components
- The full MCV IP portfolio including patent candidates (UWG, Core-Triangle, ZTAG, ACS, Wealth-Taper, Policy-as-Code, and related), trademark portfolio, copyright portfolio, and trade-secret categories
- NAOS agentic operating system design, architecture, and operational parameters
- Multi-token ecosystem design (MCV.ONE, MCV.CAP, MCV.GG, MCV.DEV, MCV.CX, MCV.TECH, MCV.DIGITAL, STATE, EDGE, BET, FORGE, GEM, and related)
- Unified Investor Journey and its 8-Stage Journey Engine
- Jurisdictional Routing Engine architecture and rule sets
- Cross-venture commercial terms and economic relationships
- Identities of all principals, partners, and counterparties of the MCV Parties
- All Tier 0, Tier 1, and Tier 2 materials under the MCV tiered disclosure architecture

**"Trade-Secret-Marked Information"** has the same meaning as in Template A.

**"Representatives"** has the same meaning as in Template A, expanded to include board members, observers, and any consultants engaged specifically to evaluate the Purpose.

**"Permitted Use"** means review and evaluation of the potential partnership, joint venture, acquisition, or commercial relationship referenced in Section 1, including related strategic planning and due-diligence activities. Permitted Use expressly does not include use for any competitive, commercial, or other purpose outside the Purpose.

**"Pre-Existing Relationship"** means any bona fide commercial relationship between Counterparty and any principal, partner, developer, or counterparty of an MCV Party that existed in writing prior to the Effective Date, including without limitation any pre-existing sales, development, or partnership relationship relating to the Cliff Bay asset. Pre-Existing Relationships are carved out of Section 8 (Non-Circumvention) to the extent of the scope of such pre-existing relationship as of the Effective Date.

### 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party obligations are identical to those in Section 3 of Template A, with the following additions reflecting the broader scope:

(h) not use Confidential Information to develop, design, or build a competing platform, token ecosystem, real-world-asset tokenization product, or agentic operating system;

(i) not knowingly interfere with the MCV Parties' relationships with principals, partners, vendors, or counterparties identified through Confidential Information, other than within the scope of Pre-Existing Relationships;

(j) at the Disclosing Party's written request, identify in writing all Representatives who have received or may reasonably be expected to receive Confidential Information.

### 4. Exclusions

Identical to Section 4 of Template A.

### 5. Compelled Disclosure

Identical to Section 5 of Template A.

### 6. Term and Survival

This Agreement shall remain in effect for **five (5) years** from the Effective Date (the "**Term**"). The confidentiality obligations in Sections 3 and 5 survive termination or expiration of this Agreement:

- for a period of **five (5) years** after termination or expiration, with respect to Confidential Information generally; and
- for a period of **seven (7) years** after termination or expiration, with respect to Trade-Secret-Marked Information; and
- indefinitely with respect to any information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, for so long as such information remains a trade secret.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

### 7. Ownership and Reservation of Rights

In addition to the ownership provisions of Section 7 of Template A, the following additional provisions apply:

(a) **No Joint IP.** Any intellectual property developed by a Receiving Party during the Term that is based on, derived from, or incorporates Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party shall be deemed to be the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party, subject to assignment on written request and without additional consideration.

(b) **Residuals Clause.** Notwithstanding any other provision, general skills, techniques, and knowledge retained in the unaided memory of a Representative (without reliance on any written, electronic, or recorded form of Confidential Information) may be used by the Receiving Party and its Representatives without violating this Agreement. For clarity, the residuals clause does not apply to Trade-Secret-Marked Information.

### 8. Non-Circumvention

For a period of **twelve (12) months** after the Effective Date, Counterparty agrees not to, directly or indirectly:

(a) contact, solicit, or transact with any principal, partner, investor, developer, vendor, or counterparty of an MCV Party (including Principal A and Principal B, if disclosed) whose identity was first learned by Counterparty through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement, for the purpose of entering into a transaction that would bypass or circumvent the MCV Parties, **except to the extent of any Pre-Existing Relationship and only within the scope of such Pre-Existing Relationship as of the Effective Date**;

(b) make an investment in, acquire rights in, or enter into a financing, tokenization, or commercialization transaction with respect to any specific asset or opportunity (including Cliff Bay) first learned of by Counterparty through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of the relevant MCV Party, except to the extent of any Pre-Existing Relationship;

(c) solicit for employment or engagement any employee or key contractor of an MCV Party whose identity was first learned by Counterparty through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of the relevant MCV Party.

This Section 8 applies mutually, with appropriate substitution of counterparty names, to any principal, partner, investor, or counterparty of Counterparty first learned of by MCV through Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement.

**Cliff Bay Carve-Out.** For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Section 8 limits any Party's continuation of a Pre-Existing Relationship relating to the Cliff Bay asset as of the Effective Date, provided such continuation is within the scope of that Pre-Existing Relationship and is not materially enhanced or expanded by reason of Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement.

### 9. Inadvertent Disclosure

Identical to Section 9 of Template A.

### 10. Remedies

An MCV Party may assign this Agreement to (i) any affiliate of an MCV Party or (ii) any Layer 0 or Layer 1 entity formed pursuant to the three-layer architecture described in the MCV Universe Counsel Engagement Onboarding Pack, upon written notice to Counterparty. On formation of the Layer 1 Singapore VCC, MCV assigns this Agreement to such VCC upon notice.

- **Publicity.** Neither Party shall make any public announcement, press release, or disclosure of the existence or terms of this Agreement, or identify the other Party as a current or prospective counterparty, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except for (i) required public-disclosure obligations under applicable securities laws, (ii) internal disclosures to Representatives bound by confidentiality, and (iii) non-material references in Counterparty's or MCV Parties' normal course corporate reporting.

- **Most-Favored-Nation Clause.** If an MCV Party enters into an NDA with a third party relating to the same FutureState partnership opportunity with materially more favorable confidentiality terms than those set forth in this Agreement, MCV shall offer those more favorable terms to Counterparty upon written request. This MFN Clause does not apply to different Purpose scopes or to counterparties in different commercial postures.

| Counterparty Type | Template | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Individual accredited investor (FutureState raise) | A | Narrow scope, 2-year sufficient |
| VC / institutional investor (FutureState raise) | A (primary), B (if broader due-diligence on full ecosystem) | A for single-venture; B if they're diligencing the platform |

## Execution Tactics

- **DocuSign or Dropbox Sign** for execution. Both Parties sign electronically. Counterpart originals stored in MCV's secured document repository.
- **Execute before sharing any Tier 1 or Tier 2 material.** Pack v2.0 is Tier 2; IP Inventory v1.1 is Tier 2. Either is shareable under either Template. Tier 1 materials (foundational codex, full technical architecture) are shareable only under Template B.
- **Do not execute either template with any party whose identity raises conflicts concerns** without first routing to engaged counsel for clearance. This is particularly relevant for Principal A and Principal B — execute only after Corp/Tax counsel has cleared conflicts on the named individuals.

## Post-Execution Filing

Keep a signed counterpart of every executed NDA in:
- MCV secure document repository (primary)
- With each MCV co-founder (redundant)
- Logged in a master NDA register with date executed, template used, counterparty, and specific scope notes

## When to Re-Execute

Re-execute either NDA when:
- The original term expires and discussions remain active (renewal with same or updated template)
- Material scope change — e.g., party initially under Template A wants to move to Template B
- Corporate change on either side — e.g., MCV Layer 1 VCC is formed and assumes MCV's NDA position (single-sided assignment is already provided for, so no re-execution needed; but counsel may recommend re-execution for institutional counterparties)
- Counsel updates the templates materially
